Matrices and Elimination

Section: 3.2 - 3.3

Echelon Form and Row Operations

A matrix is in Echelon Form (EF) if:

  • Entries below all pivots are 0.
  • Any rows of all 0s are moved to the bottom.
  • The first non-zero entry in a row is the pivot.

Elementary Row Operations

  1. Swap two rows: \(R_i \leftrightarrow R_j\)
  2. Scale a row: \(R_i \to c \cdot R_i\)
  3. Add a copy of one row to another: \(R_i \to R_i + c \cdot R_j\)

Example Reduced Echelon Form (REF):

$ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 2 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & \frac{1}{2} & 0 & -4 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -3 & -2 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} $

REF requirements: Must be in EF, all pivots scaled to 1, and entries above and below pivots are 0.

Method of (Gaussian) Elimination

  1. Step I: Eliminate variables top-down using row operations until the matrix is in Echelon Form.
  2. Step II: Substitute variables bottom-up (back substitution).

2x2 Example

System of equations:

$$ \begin{cases} x + 3y = 9 \\ 2x + y = 8 \end{cases} \Rightarrow \text{Augmented Matrix: } \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & | & 9 \\ 2 & 1 & | & 8 \end{bmatrix} $$

Operation: \(R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1\)

$$ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & | & 9 \\ 0 & -5 & | & -10 \end{bmatrix} $$

Bottom-up substitution:

  • \(-5y = -10 \Rightarrow y = 2\)
  • \(x + 3(2) = 9 \Rightarrow x = 3\)

Solution: \((x, y) = (3, 2)\)

Free Variables and General Solutions

If a column does not contain a pivot, the corresponding variable is a free variable. This lead to infinitely many solutions.

Example with Free Variable

Resulting Echelon Form:

$$ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 2 & | & 5 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 & | & 7 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix} $$

\(z\) has no pivot, so it is free. Let \(z = t\).

  • \(y + 4t = 7 \Rightarrow y = 7 - 4t\)
  • \(x - 3(7 - 4t) + 2t = 5 \Rightarrow x = 26 - 14t\)

Vector Form Solution:

$$ \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 26 \\ 7 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + t \begin{bmatrix} -14 \\ -4 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} $$

Summary of System Outcomes

Scenario Outcome
Pivot in every column (except augmented) Single / Unique Solution
Any free variables Infinitely many solutions
Pivot in the last (augmented) column (e.g., [0 0 | 1]) No solution (Inconsistent)
